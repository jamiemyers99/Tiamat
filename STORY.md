# Tiamat — Tales of the Riven Reach

## The premise

The **Riven Reach** is a green, sea-bitten land split down its eastern edge by a
great chasm called **the Riven**. Every child in the Reach knows the old song:
long ago the primordial sea-dragon **Tiamat**, the Draco Queen, was sundered, and the Reach was
built from her body — her scales became the hills, her tears the rivers, and the
Riven is the wound that never closed. She did not die. She sleeps.

Six **Wardens** keep her asleep. Each Warden guards a **Seal** — an ancient
stone set into the heart of their town — and each one runs a **Trial** for
travelling Tamers. Win a Trial and the Warden gives you their **Sigil**. Nobody
has thought much about why for a very long time.

**The Deepcall** remember. They are a quiet, robed order who believe the Reach
was stolen from the sea and that the world will only be made whole when "the
Mother" wakes and the tide takes everything back. Their leader, the
**Hierophant Oriel**, has learned the Wardens' oldest secret: the six Sigils are
the keys to the Cradle where Tiamat sleeps, and only a Tamer who has earned all
six can open it. So Oriel is not trying to beat you. Oriel is waiting for you.

## The journey

### Act I — Roots
1. **Rootmere.** You wake up on your naming day. Mum sends you to the Marsh Morph Lab.
2. **The lab.** Choose Spriglet (Nature), Cindlet (Ember) or Puddlet (Tide). Wren picks the one that beats yours and challenges you on the spot. Dr. Marsh gives you the **Index**, the **Reach Map**, capsules and a **parcel** for Warden Mossa. Mum gives you **Trail Boots** on the way out.
3. **Route 1 · Mossway** leads north to **Brindlewood**. Deliver the parcel, beat the Trial Hall, earn the **Moss Sigil** and the **Brush Hook**.
4. Side quests: find Pip, Posy's lost Burrlet, in Thornwild (reward: **Bond Charm**); Old Aldous in Brindlewood entrusts you with **Nyxen** once you hold a Sigil.

### Act II — Salt and Iron
5. **Route 2 · Bramble Bridge** and **Thornwild**: the first Deepcall acolytes are digging up an old shrine. Wren arrives, a battle, and the first mention of "the Mother".
6. **Saltreach**: the Deepcall are chartering boats for the Riven. Clear the docks, take Captain Brann's Trial, win the **Tide Sigil** and the **Skiff**. Brann explains the Wardens and the Seals.
7. **Route 3 · Gullcliff Road** and the **Coldforge Mines**: Admin Vesk is mining "scale-iron" from Tiamat's bones.
8. **Gearhollow**: the Deepcall raid the Ironworks. Help Iskra drive them out, then take her Trial for the **Spark Sigil** and the **Wing Whistle** (fly to any visited town from the Reach Map). Wren, beaten again, starts asking dangerous questions.

### Act III — Fog and Glass
9. **Route 4 · Moorwind Way** leads into fog and standing stones.
10. **Hollowmere**: Warden Morrow tells you the real legend — the Sigils are keys. Win the **Veil Sigil**. On the lake shore, Wren appears in Deepcall robes beside Maren.
11. **Route 5 · Glasslake Crossing**: launch the Skiff across the lake to the north.

### Act IV — The Riven
12. **Frostspire**: Warden Hale's Trial and the **Rime Sigil**.
13. **Route 6 · Rimepass** descends to **Riftgate**, the city on the lip of the Riven.
14. **Riftgate**: Warden Seren's Trial and the **Wyrm Sigil** — your sixth. Oriel reveals the truth: you were the key all along. Wren tries to warn you and is dragged into the **Sunken Chapel**.
15. **Sunken Chapel**: storm the Deepcall headquarters, free Wren, and follow Oriel into the **Abyssal Rift**.
16. **Tiamat's Cradle**: defeat Oriel. Tiamat wakes. With the **Covenant Capsule** forged from the six Seal-stones, you can calm her — or catch her.
17. **The Warden's Crown**: at the top of the Spire, the Wardens name you the Reach's new Covenant. Wren asks for one last battle — as equals. Credits.

## Design notes
- Levels (aces): Mossa 12, Brann 19, Iskra 26, Morrow 33, Hale 37, Seren 42, Oriel 47, Tiamat 50, Wren (final) 52.
- If you knock Tiamat out instead of catching her, she goes back to sleep in the Cradle — you can return and try again after the credits.
- `tools/balance-sim.mjs` checks every boss against a sensible team for each starter.
- Every starter has an answer to the first Trial on Route 1 (Beakling's Wing moves, Burrlet, Nibbit).
- All names in this game are original to Tiamat.
