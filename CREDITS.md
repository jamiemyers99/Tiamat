# Tiamat — Credits

**Created by** Myro, with Claude (Anthropic) as co-developer.

## Art
All pixel art is original and generated by the project's own tools in `tools/art/`
(terrain, buildings, furniture, characters, 68 Morph designs, UI, battle backgrounds,
the Reach Map and the bitmap fonts). No third-party sprite sheets, tilesets or fonts.

## Music & sound
- The original Rootmere, Route, Haven, House, wild-battle and Trial-battle themes and the
  sound effects come from the project's own audio pack (`audio/` in the earlier prototype).
- All other music and jingles are original chiptune pieces rendered by `tools/audio/songs.py`.
- Morph cries are synthesised in the browser at run time.

## Code
- Game: original code on top of [Phaser 3](https://phaser.io) (MIT) and [Vite](https://vitejs.dev) (MIT).
- Maps are written as [Tiled](https://www.mapeditor.org) JSON.

## Legal note
Tiamat is an original creature adventure inspired by the broad feel of late-1990s handheld
RPGs. It contains no Pokémon characters, names, sprites, maps, UI assets, music or sound effects.
